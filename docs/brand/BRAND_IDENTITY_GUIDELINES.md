# UPDATE CARD — faithful-source rules

## Canonical logo

The canonical UPDATE CARD logo is the stylised one-line wordmark physically present at the bottom of the supplied artwork. Its characters, geometry, order, spacing, and colours must remain exactly as supplied.

Use `brand/web/logo-horizontal.png` on white. Do not substitute an invented UC symbol, a replacement typeface, a stacked arrangement, or a recoloured rendition.

## Compact lockup

The compact lockup in `brand/logo/compact/` is cropped directly from the small UPDATE CARDS lockup in the supplied artwork. It is not a newly designed icon.

## Backgrounds and formats

The source file has a white background. Use the `*-on-white.png` files unchanged where the background is light or create a white container behind them on darker surfaces. The transparent derivatives are only for light surfaces and have not been recoloured.

## Current boundary

The source provided is a 1080×876 JPEG. It is not sufficient to claim a clean vector master, a redesigned monochrome logo, a favicon, or large app icons without changing the original. These formats are intentionally withheld pending the original vector or high-resolution transparent logo asset.

## Do not

Do not redraw, stretch, skew, rotate, recolour, split into a new composition, replace the custom lettering, or use the previously generated UC symbol. Any new variant must first be supplied or approved as an original source asset.
