# UPDATE CARD — Page Experience Architecture v1.0

**Status:** APPROVED CREATIVE / PAGE-EXPERIENCE DIRECTION  
**Scope:** Visual composition and content-experience direction for the core public website pages.  
**Depends on:** `WEB_DESIGN_ENGINEERING_STANDARD.md`, `SITE_MAP_AND_PAGE_ARCHITECTURE_V1.md`, `SERVICE_TAXONOMY_V1.md`, `COMPANY_PROFILE_CONTENT_BASELINE.md`.

> This document does not replace the general web engineering standard or the site map. It defines how the approved pages should feel, flow, and differ visually while remaining one coherent system.

---

## 1. Core creative direction

UPDATE CARD is an informational multi-page brand/profile website, **not an online store**.

The experience should feel:

- Digital
- Editorial
- Modern
- Comfortable
- Professional
- Human-friendly
- Visually distinctive without sacrificing clarity

The goal is not to copy the common pattern of:

`Hero → equal cards → counters → generic why-us → CTA`

The website should feel **art-directed**, while interaction patterns remain familiar and easy to understand.

### Global visual principles

- Strong Arabic typography and hierarchy.
- Generous whitespace and comfortable reading rhythm.
- Controlled alternation between light, dark and branded surfaces.
- Service imagery is a major visual storytelling tool, not decoration.
- Avoid a grid of identical cards across the whole site.
- Use composition, scale, cropping, negative space and image direction to create hierarchy.
- Use gradients, 3D, glow and decorative motion only when they add meaning.
- No generic gaming-store neon aesthetic.
- No stock-photo dependency.
- No random collage of platform logos.
- No auto-playing carousel.
- Each page has a distinct composition appropriate to its content, while sharing the same design system.

### Brand motif

Create one restrained reusable visual motif derived from the geometry/motion language of the UC mark.

Possible uses:

- crop/framing device
- directional line
- mask
- section divider
- subtle background texture

It must support the layout rather than become decoration noise.

# 3. Home page `/`

## Purpose

Create the strongest first impression of UPDATE CARD, communicate service breadth, explain the brand briefly and route visitors toward deeper service exploration or direct contact.

The home page is a **summary and discovery page**, not the place for complete service details.

## 3.1 Opening / Hero

Avoid the traditional full-screen hero with a generic laptop, floating logos or heavy decorative graphics.

Recommended composition:

- Clear primary message
- Very short brand introduction
- Primary CTA: explore services
- Secondary CTA: WhatsApp contact
- One crafted brand visual representing the digital ecosystem

Visual concept may combine abstract references to:

- gaming
- digital cards
- payments
- subscriptions
- international shopping

Do not turn this into a collage of third-party logos.

The hero image/artwork will be produced later as a dedicated website asset with desktop/mobile crops.

## 3.2 UPDATE CARD introduction

Use an editorial section instead of a generic "About us" card.

Key visual fact:

**2018**

paired with a concise statement about experience in digital services.

End with a simple link to `/about/`.

Do not repeat the full About page.

## 3.3 Service world / main service families

This should be one of the strongest visual sections on the site.

Use an **editorial/Bento service composition**, not eight equal cards.

Main families:

1. الألعاب والشحن
2. التطبيقات الاجتماعية والترفيهية
3. البطاقات الرقمية
4. الاشتراكات
5. البرمجيات والتراخيص والحماية
6. الدفع الإلكتروني والأصول الرقمية والمحافظ
7. الشراء من المواقع العالمية والتوصيل لليمن
8. خدمة حسب الطلب

Each service-family block should include:

- Art-directed image
- Category name
- One short supporting line
- Clear exploration link

Different blocks may have different proportions based on hierarchy, but the grid must remain coherent and responsive.

## 3.4 Custom request feature

Do not hide Custom Request inside the main grid only.

Give it a distinct follow-up section with the concept:

**لم تجد الخدمة التي تبحث عنها؟**

and a clear explanation that the customer can send the request for availability review.

This section should visually differ from the standard service imagery.

## 3.5 Retail and wholesale

The business supports both retail and wholesale customers.

Use a split editorial composition rather than generic feature cards:

- للأفراد
- لقطاع الجملة

Do not invent wholesale benefits, pricing or terms until the business provides them.

## 3.6 How dealing with UPDATE CARD works

Avoid three equal cards with numbers.

Use a lightweight flow such as:

**استكشف → تواصل → نراجع طلبك → يتم التنفيذ**

Exact wording can be refined later.

Use typography and micro-visual cues rather than boxed cards.

## 3.7 Trust / proof

Use only confirmed facts:

- Since 2018
- Retail and wholesale
- Multiple digital service families
- Direct communication

Do not publish fabricated customer counts, satisfaction rates or unsupported badges.

## 3.8 Final CTA

Create a strong branded closing surface rather than a generic green banner.

Concept:

**عندك خدمة في بالك؟**

Then a clear WhatsApp action, followed by the shared Footer.

# 6. Service-family pages

Common URLs include:

- `/services/games/`
- `/services/social-entertainment/`
- `/services/gift-cards/`
- `/services/subscriptions/`
- `/services/software-licenses/`
- `/services/digital-payments/`
- `/services/international-shopping/`
- `/services/custom-request/`

## Shared system

All service-family pages share:

- Header/Footer
- Breadcrumb system
- Typography
- Image ratios/tokens
- Service card component family
- CTA system
- Responsive spacing

But they must **not look like cloned pages**.

Each family may have a different visual rhythm appropriate to the subject while preserving the same design system.

### Visual personality by category

**Games**  
More energy and image emphasis, but no cheap neon/cyberpunk overload.

**Social & entertainment**  
Lighter, more social and expressive visual rhythm.

**Gift cards**  
Clean editorial/product-style presentation.

**Subscriptions**  
Entertainment-oriented mood with restrained platform visual references.

**Software & licenses**  
Minimal, technical and structured.

**Digital payments**  
Calm, trustworthy, precise. Avoid speculative financial imagery and flashy crypto clichés.

**International shopping**  
Editorial shopping/package/global-market visual storytelling.

## Service item cards

Individual games/services may use **image-led cards**.

A card may contain:

- Purpose-made service image/artwork
- Service/platform name
- Very short verified description
- Clear detail or contact action

Do not reduce every item to a logo on a colored square.

Do not imply that UPDATE CARD is the official operator of third-party brands unless that relationship is verified.

Individual detail pages should only be created where sufficient content and/or SEO value exists.

# 10. Image system and future asset generation

Images are a core part of the design direction.

Do not generate isolated images randomly.

Create one **Art Direction system** for the complete family of assets.

Initial asset families to define later:

1. Home hero visual
2. Games category
3. Social & entertainment category
4. Gift cards category
5. Subscriptions category
6. Software/licenses category
7. Digital payments category
8. International shopping category
9. Custom-request visual
10. Individual high-priority game/service cards

For each asset define before generation:

- purpose
- aspect ratio
- subject
- composition
- lighting/style
- brand integration
- desktop crop
- mobile crop
- text-safe area if required

### Web delivery

Generated raster assets must be optimized for production:

- AVIF/WebP preferred where appropriate
- responsive `srcset` / `sizes`
- explicit dimensions
- lazy-load non-critical images
- do not lazy-load the primary LCP image
- keep original/source assets outside production delivery when heavy

Use SVG for logos and simple vector marks rather than raster images.

# 11. Motion direction

Motion must be functional and restrained.

Appropriate examples:

- small hover transitions
- image crop/scale response on service cards
- subtle section reveal where it does not delay reading
- directional motion linked to the UC motif

Avoid:

- constant floating
- large parallax stacks
- auto-scrolling carousels
- heavy 3D scenes
- animations that block content

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

---

# 12. Responsive behavior

Mobile is not a shrunk desktop layout.

For mobile:

- Preserve hierarchy, not exact geometry
- Bento layouts may collapse/reorder intentionally
- Images must retain useful focal points
- CTAs remain easy to reach
- Avoid horizontal scrolling
- Avoid fixed heights that clip Arabic text
- Keep tap targets comfortable

Desktop and mobile should feel like the same art direction expressed appropriately for each viewport.

---

# 13. Anti-template / anti-AI-look rules

Reject designs that depend on:

- identical cards repeated section after section
- unnecessary glowing gradients
- fake glass panels everywhere
- random 3D coins/devices
- generic stock business people
- decorative platform-logo clouds
- arbitrary circles/blob backgrounds
- long marketing copy with no informational value
- sections added only because standard templates have them

The design should be recognizable through composition, typography, imagery and the UC-derived motif — not through effects.

---

# 14. Implementation rule

The website must use one shared design system and reusable components, but **reuse must not force every page into the same composition**.

Principle:

> Same function = same component behavior.  
> Different content purpose = composition may differ.

Do not duplicate markup/CSS for visually identical elements. Do not over-abstract one-off compositions into unnecessary component layers.
